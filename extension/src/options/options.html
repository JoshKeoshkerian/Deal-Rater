Deal Rater

Captures the Marketplace vehicle listing you are viewing, when you ask it to.

Please read before using this.

Collecting listing data from Facebook Marketplace with a tool is against Meta's terms of service, whether or not it runs in your own browser under your own session. Facebook may restrict or suspend your account for it. That risk falls on you, not on this extension.

This extension acts only when you click Capture. It does not run searches on its own, does not monitor listings in the background, and does nothing at all while you are just browsing.

Marketplace shows asking prices, not sale prices. Anything built on this data describes how vehicles are advertised, not what they sell for.

What is stored about sellers

Two things, and only two: an irreversible hash of the seller's Facebook ID, and a count of their active vehicle listings. Names, profile links, profile photos and join dates are never read or sent. Phone numbers and email addresses in a listing description are replaced with [PHONE] and [EMAIL] before the description leaves your browser.

Settings

Saved fixtures contain the whole page, including personal data. Scrub them with npm run scrub-fixture before committing.

Nearby markets

Facebook's search radius is fixed at 40 miles and cannot be changed per search, so the only way to widen the comparison is to search neighbouring metros separately. Each line here adds one more search per capture.

To find an id: open Marketplace, switch the location to that city, and copy the number out of the address bar — facebook.com/marketplace/108013345886344/search?…
